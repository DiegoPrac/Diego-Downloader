RESPONSIBLE USE

Use Diego Downloader for content you are allowed to save.

Diego Downloader is intended for personal media, public-domain content, creator-authorized downloads, and other media that you have permission or a legal right to save.

The application is not designed to access private accounts or bypass login restrictions and access controls.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Does Diego Downloader use an online converter website?

No. The Windows application is designed to process supported media locally.

Can the result be saved to a flash drive?

Yes. Users can choose a normal folder or a connected removable drive.

What audio quality is used?

The standard MP3 option currently uses 128 kbps.

Does every online media link work?

No. It depends on the source, public access, technical support, and the permission to access the content.
